A surrogate matters only when it works inside a decision loop.

EdgeGenBench is a reproducible benchmark for hybrid-electric and hydrogen regional-aircraft design. It evaluates predictive accuracy together with uncertainty, safety-aware feasibility decisions, optimization behavior, physics validation, and deployment equivalence.

Benchmark design

The v0.1 benchmark starts with 6,000 deterministic synthetic aircraft-design samples spanning passenger capacity, design range, cruise speed, battery specific energy, hydrogen storage efficiency, hybridization ratio, and propulsion architecture.

Ridge, Random Forest, and HistGradientBoosting surrogates are compared under reproducible train, validation, calibration, and held-out test partitions.

HistGradientBoosting provides the strongest classical predictive accuracy, while Random Forest is retained for uncertainty estimation, optimization, and ONNX export.

Uncertainty and safety-aware feasibility

The benchmark combines Random Forest tree-quantile intervals with split-conformal prediction and a feasibility classifier whose operating point is selected with explicit attention to false-safe errors.

The ordinary validation threshold is not assumed to remain safe under optimizer-selected data. A separate physics-validated optimization acceptance threshold is therefore used for downstream design selection.

Optimization inside the model loop

Latin-hypercube sampling generates 20,000 candidate designs. The constrained optimization workflow accepts 5,413 candidates, extracts 47 Pareto designs, and selects representative solutions for direct physics-model checks.

The reported Pareto-front and representative-design feasibility agreement is 100% for the current benchmark configuration.

ONNX deployment and equivalence

The Random Forest surrogate and feasibility classifier are exported to ONNX and checked against their scikit-learn counterparts before latency claims are made. Classifier decision agreement is 100%, with a maximum probability error of 1.82 × 10⁻⁷.

At batch size 1, surrogate latency improves from 13.795 ms in scikit-learn to 0.305 ms in ONNX Runtime; classifier latency improves from 14.787 ms to 0.311 ms.

Scope

EdgeGenBench v0.1 does not include a neural surrogate, INT8 quantization, a Design Review Agent, mobile GPU/NPU benchmarking, or production aircraft data. Those are roadmap directions rather than current implementation claims.
