AeroRAG-X: evidence-grounded RAG for technical literature

Independent RAG system over public NASA technical literature, combining BM25 and dense retrieval, Reciprocal Rank Fusion, cross-encoder reranking, pgvector, evidence-sufficiency checks, citation-preserving generation, adaptive retrieval, and structured FastAPI services.

Defined evaluation suite: semantic concept coverage improved from 38.2% to 51.3%, and full answer-to-claim capture from 10% to 45%, while testing evidence support and safe non-assertion on unsupported queries.

EdgeGenBench: uncertainty-aware scientific ML and efficient inference

Reproducible benchmark for hybrid-electric and hydrogen aircraft design, connecting multi-output surrogate models, uncertainty estimation, feasibility classification, constrained optimization, physics validation, ONNX export, quantization, and a bounded Design Review Agent.

Current benchmark: 0.995 mean held-out test R2 and 99.89% ROC AUC, with accuracy, uncertainty, safety, model-size, latency, and throughput trade-offs made explicit.

NewsLens: leakage-aware recommendation, search, and serving

End-to-end ML system over 5.84M candidate interactions using chronological evaluation, TF-IDF personalization, a training-only popularity fallback, bootstrap confidence intervals, subgroup and failure analysis, FastAPI, Docker, CI/CD, and observability.

Held-out evaluation: NDCG@10 0.3664, Recall@10 0.5955, and Hit Rate@10 0.6762.

Independent - Jul 2026

Case studyCode

Failure detection in time-series thrust data

M.S. thesis research on Scientific ML and applied ML for simulation reliability. Audited a 15,120-case rocket-motor campaign, identified 6,804 candidate simulator failures, removed duplicate-geometry leakage, and evaluated the final classifier on unseen geometries.

Held-out result: 96.8% accuracy and ROC AUC 0.986, replicated on an independent 1,050-case study.
