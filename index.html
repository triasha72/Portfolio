NURBS-BEM: numerical electromagnetics and operator learning

Mesh-free electromagnetic solver built from the integral formulation through code, with STEP-to-NURBS geometry processing, a two-fidelity simulation dataset, closed-form verification, and a GNN–DeepONet/SIREN surrogate pipeline.

The first neural training run failed silently after ~11 GPU-hours; gradient and activation diagnostics exposed input-scale overflow, disconnected parameters, and a physics-loss defect. The corrected pipeline is implemented; retraining remains intentionally unclaimed.

Audited a large solid-rocket-motor simulation campaign, identified 6,804 silent failures, removed duplicate-geometry leakage, and evaluated failure classification on unseen geometries.
